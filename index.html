Our Mission

We fund patient-centered research and unite scientists, clinicians, and families to uncover answers and develop meaningful therapies for SMG9 deficiency.

🔬

Advancing Research

Driving scientific discovery to better understand SMG9 deficiency and develop meaningful treatments.

👨‍👩‍👧

Supporting Families

Providing resources, connection, and compassionate support for families navigating life with SMG9 deficiency.

💙

Building Hope

Together, we are creating a future defined by answers, treatments, and hope for every individual affected.

Research Focus

SMG9 deficiency is a rare genetic condition. We support research aimed at understanding its mechanisms and accelerating the path toward effective therapies.

Gene Therapy Approaches

Exploring promising strategies including AAV-based delivery and readthrough approaches that could address the underlying genetic cause.

Disease Models

Supporting the development of cellular and animal models that enable researchers to study SMG9 function and test potential treatments.

Collaborative Science

Working with academic partners and research institutions to share knowledge and accelerate progress for this rare condition.

For Families

You are not alone. We are committed to supporting families affected by SMG9 deficiency with information, connection, and advocacy.

Whether you are newly diagnosed or have been navigating this journey for years, our goal is to help you access reliable information and connect with others who understand.
